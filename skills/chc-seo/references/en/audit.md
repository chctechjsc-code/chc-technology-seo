# CHC Technology — Full website audit

Apply SKILL.md and the language policy. This is a CHC adaptation, not a line-by-line translation.

1. Identify business type, target markets and representative page templates. Inspect the homepage, sitemap, robots and internal links; disclose tested URL count and sampling coverage.
2. Combine technical, content, on-page, schema, performance, images, GEO and SXO findings; add local, commerce and international checks when relevant.
3. Call this a full-site audit only after inspecting representative URLs or reliable crawl data. When using a crawler, disclose its URL cap, rate, timeout and robots policy; back off on 429/503. The bundled CHC CLI checks one page, robots.txt and up to three sitemaps.
4. Deduplicate root causes and prioritize crawl/indexing blockers. Deliver a report, action plan and evidence records.
