# CHC Technology — Backlink analysis

Apply SKILL.md and the language policy. This is a CHC adaptation, not a line-by-line translation.

1. Identify source, timestamp and coverage; a crawl sample is not a complete backlink profile.
2. Analyze referring domains, anchors, targets, rel attributes and relevance.
3. Verify important links and distinguish removed links, redirects and access failures.
4. Do not infer penalties from spam scores or default to disavow. Deliver relevant opportunities with evidence.
