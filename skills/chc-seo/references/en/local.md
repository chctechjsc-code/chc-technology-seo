# CHC Technology — Local SEO

Apply SKILL.md and the language policy. This is a CHC adaptation, not a line-by-line translation.

1. Identify storefront, service-area or hybrid businesses.
2. Cross-check name, address, phone, hours, service area and GBP against confirmed business data.
3. Review local service pages, authentic reviews, citations, links and suitable schema.
4. Never invent branches or addresses; do not assume US directories fit Vietnam.
